A few helpful answers

Before your
first visit.

How do I arrange a consultation?

Open the nursing home’s Google listing using the contact button below, then use the contact details shown there to confirm consultation availability and booking arrangements.

Which services are available?

Please check directly with MB Nursing Home for the current list of departments, services, and visiting consultants.

What should I bring with me?

Bring identification and any relevant previous medical reports, prescriptions, and a current medication list. Ask the nursing home if anything else is needed for your visit.

Can a family member accompany me?

Check with the nursing home before arriving to confirm attendant arrangements and current visiting hours.
